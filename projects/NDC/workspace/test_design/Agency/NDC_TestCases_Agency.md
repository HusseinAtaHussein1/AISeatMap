# Create Agency

## TC-AGENCY-CREATE-001 — Create Agency: full valid submission

**Objective:** Verify a fully valid Agency submission succeeds and enters Pending approval, not Active.

**Preconditions:** User is logged in as Admin. An existing Branch, Supplier/PCC record, and Payment Method reference data exist.

**Test Data:**
- Branch: any existing Branch. Agency Name: `QA_SPIKE_{n}`.
- State/City: valid pair. Post Office Box: `12345`. Phone No.: national number, no `+` prefix.
- Address 1: any valid string.
- Owner Contact Person/Email/Phone: valid values.
- Document: any PDF + Contract Start/End Date. Logo: any valid image ≤2MB.
- Sales Contact: Name/Email/Phone.
- Payment Settings: Invoice Generation, Credit Limit, Top-Up Limit, Credit Terms (Days), at least one Payment Option.
- Product Access: one Product selected. Airline Supplier Credential PCC: Search/Generic Supplier PCC, Manager Name, Description.
- Import PNR Option: On.

**Steps:**
1. Open Add Agency.
2. Select Branch.
3. Enter Agency Name, State, City, Post Office Box, Address 1, Phone No.
4. Fill Owner Details (Contact Person, Email, Phone).
5. Upload a document and set Contract Start/End Date.
6. Upload a logo.
7. Fill Sales Contact (Name, Email, Phone).
8. Fill Payment Settings (Invoice Generation, Credit Limit, Top-Up Limit, Credit Terms, one Payment Option).
9. Select Product Access.
10. Fill Airline Supplier Credential PCC details.
11. Toggle Import PNR Option On.
12. Click **Send For Approval**.

**Expected Result:** The Agency is created successfully and enters `Pending for approval`, not `Active`.

**Priority:** P1
**Tags:** Scenario, Smoke, Positive, AutomationCandidate
